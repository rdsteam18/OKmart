OK Mart — FAQ ❓

Ahmedabad me 15-minute grocery delivery ke sawaal-jawaab.

📍 Delivery Areas — Ahmedabad

OK Mart delivers in Jantanagar, Satellite, Prahlad Nagar and nearby areas of Ahmedabad. Apna pincode homepage par check karo — serviceable areas me 15 minute me delivery milti hai.

OK Mart kya hai?

OK Mart Ahmedabad ka 15-minute grocery delivery store hai — okmart.netlify.app par milk, sabzi, phal, snacks aur daily essentials order karo, 15 minute me ghar tak delivery.

Delivery kitni der me aati hai?

Lagbhag 15 minute me, serviceable areas me. Order karne ke baad aapko tracking link milta hai.

Delivery charge kitna hai? Free delivery kab milti hai?

₹199 se upar ke orders par FREE delivery milti hai. Usse kam ke orders par chhota delivery charge lagta hai, jo checkout par dikhta hai.

Payment kaise kar sakte hain?

COD (Cash on Delivery), UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm) aur delivery par QR code — teeno options available hain.

Order kaise karein?

Website par location chuno, cart me samaan daalo, address daalo aur Place Order dabao. Ya WhatsApp par order karo: +91 99822 39821.

Daily Loot Deals kya hain?

Roz naye price-drop deals — Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Ajio ke top-brand products bade discount par. Deals page par roz 5 naye deals aate hain.

Refer & Earn kaise kaam karta hai?

Apna referral link padosi ko bhejo — unke pehle order par unko ₹20 OFF apne aap lagega, aur aapko bhi ₹20 reward milega agle order ke liye. Apna code Profile > Refer & Earn me milega (Google login ke baad).

Kya login zaroori hai?

Nahi — guest ke roop me bhi order kar sakte ho. Google login se order history, saved addresses aur Refer & Earn code milta hai.

Order track kaise karein?

Order ke baad mile tracking link se, ya Track Order page par order id daal kar.
