Privacy Policy

📅 Last updated: November 3, 2026

Introduction

OK Mart is a neighbourhood grocery delivery service in Ahmedabad, run by RDS TEAM. This policy explains, in plain language, what personal data we collect from you, why we collect it, how long we keep it, and the rights you have over it under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the DPDP Rules, 2025.

1. Data we collect

Only what is needed to deliver your groceries:

• Name and phone number — to identify your orders and contact you about delivery.
• Email address — only if you provide it, or when you sign in with Google.
• Delivery addresses and location — the address you save, and your GPS location only when you choose or confirm it on the map.
• Order history — what you ordered, so you can reorder and we can help with support.
• Referral and wallet records — your referral code, wallet balance and credits, so your rewards work correctly.
• Notification token — only if you tap "Enable" on the order-updates prompt, so we can send delivery updates.
• Google profile details — name, email and photo, only if you choose "Continue with Google".

We do not collect payment card details on this site. Cash on Delivery is handled in person, and online payments are processed by the payment option you choose at checkout.

2. Why we use it

Your data is used only for these purposes: processing and delivering your orders, arranging delivery and customer support, applying referral and wallet credits, sending order updates and offers (only if you enabled notifications), and improving the service. We never sell your personal data, and we never use it for any purpose other than the ones listed here without your consent.

3. Who we share it with

We share your order details only where delivery needs it: your order is sent to our WhatsApp order channel so our team can fulfil it. The site itself is hosted on Google Firebase, which stores the data described above. Nobody else receives your personal data — no advertisers, no data brokers, no other third parties.

4. Your consent

By creating an account, signing in, or placing an order, you consent to the uses described in this policy. You can withdraw your consent at any time — contact us (see section 8) or raise a data deletion request from your Profile page, and we will stop using your data except where the law requires us to keep a record.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your profile, addresses and order history while your account is active, so reordering and support keep working. When you ask us to delete your data and we verify the request, we delete your personal details and saved addresses. Order records needed for accounts and legal reasons may be kept in anonymised form, without your name, phone or address attached.

6. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can: see what personal data we hold about you; correct it anytime from your Profile page (name, phone, email, saved addresses); ask us to delete it through the data deletion request on your Profile page; withdraw your consent; and raise a grievance with us first, and then with the Data Protection Board of India if you remain unsatisfied. You may also nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf — tell us in your request.

7. Children's data

OK Mart is a service for adults (18 years and above). We do not target children, and we do not track children or show them targeted advertising. If you are under 18, please use OK Mart only with your parent or guardian's consent.

8. Security and breaches

We use reasonable security safeguards to protect your data. No system is perfect: if a personal data breach occurs that affects you, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires, and tell you what we are doing about it.

9. Contact and grievances

For any privacy question, correction, consent withdrawal or deletion request, reach us on WhatsApp at +91 9982239821. We will respond to your request after verifying that it comes from you.

This is a plain-language summary of our data practices. If any part of this policy changes, the updated date at the top will change too. This policy is written to follow the DPDP Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025; it is not legal advice.
