📋 Introduction

OK Mart is an online grocery delivery platform serving selected areas. By using our website, you agree to the following terms and conditions. These terms are designed to ensure a smooth and fair experience for all our customers.

🛒 Order Policy

  • All orders are confirmed via WhatsApp message to your registered phone number.
  • Product availability may change based on real-time stock.
  • Some items may be replaced with similar products of equal or higher value if unavailable.
  • If a replacement is not acceptable, the item may be cancelled from your order.
  • We reserve the right to limit quantities on certain products.

🚚 Delivery Policy

  • Delivery is available only in selected pincodes. You can check serviceability on the checkout page.
  • Delivery time may vary depending on demand, traffic, weather conditions, and other factors.
  • Our standard delivery time is 10-15 minutes, but during peak hours it may take longer.
  • Please ensure someone is available to receive the order at the provided address.
  • If no one is available, the order may be returned and a redelivery charge may apply.

🏪 Store Pickup Policy

If you choose store pickup, delivery charges will not be applied. The delivery charge shown in the bill will be removed during final confirmation.

  • Store pickup orders must be collected within 2 hours of confirmation.
  • Please bring your order confirmation (WhatsApp message) when picking up.
  • Unclaimed orders after 24 hours may be cancelled without refund.

🥛 Dairy Products Limit

Dairy products are limited per day. A maximum of 1–2 dairy items per order will be fulfilled. Any additional dairy items may be cancelled.

  • This limit applies to items like milk, butter, cheese, paneer, and yogurt.
  • The limit helps ensure fair distribution and freshness for all customers.
  • If you need larger quantities, please contact us on WhatsApp for special arrangements.

🏷️ Offers & Free Items

  • All offers and discounts are valid for a limited time only.
  • Minimum order conditions apply for free delivery and promotional items.
  • Free items (such as free onion on orders above ₹199) are only added if all conditions are met.
  • OK Mart reserves the right to modify or cancel any offer without prior notice.
  • Offers cannot be combined unless explicitly stated.

❌ Cancellation & Refund Policy

  • Orders can be cancelled before dispatch by contacting us on WhatsApp.
  • Once the order is out for delivery, cancellation may not be possible.
  • Refund requests must be made via WhatsApp with your Order ID.
Valid reasons for refund:
❌ Wrong Item 💔 Damaged Item 📦 Missing Item 🥛 Expired Product

Refunds will be processed within 24-48 hours after verification.

👤 User Responsibility

  • Provide correct and complete address and phone number for delivery.
  • Be available to receive the order at the scheduled delivery time.
  • Verify all items at the time of delivery and report any issues immediately.
  • Keep your account and order information confidential.
